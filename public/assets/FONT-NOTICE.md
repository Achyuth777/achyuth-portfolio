# AU Grotesk

Latin subsets of TeX Gyre Heros Regular and Bold, downloaded from https://tug.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre/opentype/texgyreheros-regular.otf and texgyreheros-bold.otf. Original family: TeX Gyre Heros; designers Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki, GUST e-foundry.

Modified for this portfolio: subset characters, convert OTF to WOFF2, rename family/PostScript names to AU Grotesk. Font copyright metadata is retained.
